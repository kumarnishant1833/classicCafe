FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and timings.

What are your opening hours? ▼
We are open every day until 10:00 PM. You can visit us any day of the week for all your printing, scanning, and digital assistance needs.
Where are you located exactly? ▼
We are located at M38, Indralok Market, Near Public School, Kanpur Road, Manas Nagar, Lucknow – 226023, Uttar Pradesh. We are easy to find near the Public School on Kanpur Road.
Do you help with Aadhaar card updates and PAN card applications? ▼
Yes! We provide full assistance with Aadhaar updates (address, mobile number, etc.) and PAN card applications or corrections. Please bring your relevant documents.
Can I get colour printouts here? ▼
Absolutely. We offer both black & white and colour printouts at very affordable rates. We also do photocopies of documents.
Do you help with passport form filling? ▼
Yes, we assist with passport application form filling, document checklist preparation, and online portal submission guidance.
Can I book train or bus tickets here? ▼
Yes, we provide ticket booking assistance for trains and buses. Just come in with your travel details and we'll help you book quickly.
Do you provide lamination services? ▼
Yes, we offer lamination for certificates, ID cards, important documents and more to keep them safe and long-lasting.
